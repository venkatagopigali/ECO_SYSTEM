DATE FUNCTIONS :-- will used to perform operation on date type of data

now()                        :-- will return present date and time  
current_date() or curdate()  :-- will return present date
current_time() or curtime()  :-- will return present time
date_add('date',interval count day/month/year) :--
		>>>will used to add no.of days or months or year to date
date_sub('date',interval count day/month/year) :--
		>>>will used to subtract no.of days or months or years
datediff('end_date','start_date')  :--
		>>>will used to return the no.of days in between two dates
extract(year/month/day/hour/minute/second from 'date') :
		>>>will extract year or month or day or minutes or hours or seconds from date
day()          :-- will extract day from date
month()        :-- will extract month from date
year()         :-- will extract year from date
hour()         :-- will extract hour from date
minute()       :-- will extract minute from date
second()       :-- will extract second from date
dayname()      :-- will return name of the day
monthname()    :-- will return name of the month
